How to read these numbers

Revenue counts money taken in this month (by payment date) and is gross — refunds are not subtracted from it.

Refunds paid out is money that left this month; a refund may belong to a booking paid in an earlier month.

The status table follows the bookings created this month and shows where each one stands today, so it can change after the month closes.

Revenue recognised counts trips that finished this month, so it differs from cash collected — money for a December trip is taken today but earned in December. Money kept from a cancelled booking counts as revenue too.

Per-traveller costs follow the travellers who went, including anyone given a full goodwill refund; per-departure costs are charged once for each departure that ran with at least one traveller.

Net profit is after cost of sales, tax and payment fees. It does not include salaries, rent or marketing.

Cancellations are counted on the day they were made. Within the deadline means on or before the free-cancellation deadline of the trip (Vietnam time).
