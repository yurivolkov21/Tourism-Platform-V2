Booking flow

Three screens and their states, built from the shipped system: Wuling tokens straight out of tokens.mjs, Literata over Archivo with IBM Plex Mono for references, and the primitives that already exist. Payment always leaves for the provider's own page — there is no card field anywhere in this flow, and every checkout runs in sandbox mode.

Notes

What to check

The four things worth arguing about before this becomes code, and two measurements.

Status is never one badge

The five words appear once each, in monospace, as the system's vocabulary. Every screen that shows one also shows two cells — Trip and Payment — that answer separately. Two CANCELLED frames prove the point: same word, opposite money story.

No card fields, no deposit

Payment is a choice of destination, not a form. Stripe or PayPal, then off to their hosted page for the full amount. The words "deposit" and "card number" appear nowhere.

Jade dosage

Primary carries the hero texture, the selected row, the radio dot and the one button that moves things forward. Everything else is mist, celadon and ink. Amber appears only on "Only 3 seats left"; the rating gold is only ever a star.

Components, all from the 63

  • The party counters are button-group + input, not the stepper primitive — that one is for multi-step flows, and this page is deliberately not one.
  • Everything else: field, input, textarea, radio-group, toggle-group, item, badge, card, button, separator, breadcrumb, collapsible, empty, spinner, alert.
  • Nothing new to write.

Two contrast findings from building this

  • text-warning on a card measures 2.01:1 in light — that is what departures-table.tsx currently uses for "Only n seats left". Here it became a pale pill with ink text instead: 12.98:1.
  • In dark, destructive on a card is 2.83:1 and primary is 2.58:1 — so field errors use a tinted strip with ink text rather than data-invalid:text-destructive, and links use accent-foreground (7.7:1) rather than primary.

Where the numbers come from

Tour, prices, group size and destinations are the real North to South Classic and Sa Pa Homestay Weekend fixtures. References follow BK- + 8 base36 characters. The 65-minute expiry and the "refunded to date" figure match the pending sweep and the refund ledger.
